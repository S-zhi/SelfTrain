# Third-party UI notices

The answer-choice card, primary button and progress styling in `src/styles.css` adapt these Uiverse community components:

- Yaya12085, [quiz radio card](https://github.com/uiverse-io/galaxy/blob/main/Radio-buttons/Yaya12085_empty-badger-73.html). The original three-choice card and CSS-only correct-answer detection were replaced with four semantic radio choices and feedback driven by the server.
- SteveBloX, [raised button](https://github.com/uiverse-io/galaxy/blob/main/Buttons/SteveBloX_light-owl-27.html). Colors, dimensions and focus states were adapted.
- andrew-demchenk0 (source credit in the snippet: Frank Esteban), [card progress element](https://github.com/uiverse-io/galaxy/blob/main/Cards/andrew-demchenk0_chilly-bird-79.html). Only the native progress treatment is adapted; its avatars and decorative card are not used.
